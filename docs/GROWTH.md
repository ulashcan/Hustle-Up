# Growth

Not a feature dump. The product still has to make better moves. These are hypotheses to test, not claims.

## Store listing (draft — do not ship unsupported claims)

- **Name:** HUSTLE UP
- **Subtitle:** Know your edge. Make the move.
- **Promise:** Screenshot or type the situation. Get a move — not a speech.
- **Keywords to test:** negotiation, screenshot, reply, offer, fumble, practice, edge
- **Do not claim:** “AI-powered personal assistant,” market rates, guaranteed outcomes, dating-app identity.

Icon: near-black field, ember mark, no chat bubble.

## Creative testing system

Treat every ad as an experiment. One variable at a time.

| Slot | What to vary |
| --- | --- |
| Hook (0–2s) | First line + first frame + on-screen caption |
| Problem | Work / money / conflict / reply — not only dating |
| Visual | Phone-in-hand screen record vs talking head |
| Reveal | Fumble first vs MOVE first |
| CTA | WHAT'S THE MOVE? vs DON'T FUMBLE |

Judge hook rate and hold rate before CPI. Do not pick a “viral” winner from one post.

## Pattern library (extracted, not copied)

Current short-form ads that hold attention tend to:

1. Open on the problem, not the logo.
2. Land a hook in the first 1–2 seconds (spoken + caption + frame).
3. Drop the viewer into a POV or mid-action, not a brand intro.
4. Show the product as proof — screen record, not a slogan.
5. Close a curiosity loop before the CTA.

## HUSTLE UP concepts to shoot

**A — Fumble interrupt**

- Hook: “Bro was about to send THIS.”
- Show the bad draft.
- “HUSTLE UP stopped him.”
- Fumble: “You’re negotiating against yourself.”
- MOVE: COUNTER + one line to send.
- End: WHAT'S THE MOVE?

**B — Screenshot in**

- First frame: a real-looking offer text (staged).
- Overlay: “What’s the move?”
- Cut to COUNTER, not a chatbot thread.

**C — Silence is a move**

- Hook: “She left you on read. You’re about to double text.”
- WAIT. Don’t send.
- End: DON’T FUMBLE.

Shoot work and money versions of A before any dating-only cut. Dating is one scenario, not the brand.

## What we will not do

- Chat-first ads.
- “Your AI wingman.”
- Fake leverage percentages on screen.
- Social feeds, likes, or public profiles in the product to chase virality.
